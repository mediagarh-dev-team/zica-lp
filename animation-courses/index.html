PROGRAMS

A 27-Month Journey From
Fundamentals to Specialisation

Progress through five structured modules, developing your creative and technical skills from the basics to advanced animation and VFX specialisation.

MODULE 01

Fundamentals of Art, Design & Computer Graphics

Build your creative foundation through drawing, colour theory, storytelling, character design, storyboarding and computer graphics.

6 MONTHS
MODULE 02

3D Digital Art

Explore 3D modelling, environmental creation, advanced texturing, rendering and look development.

6 MONTHS
MODULE 03

Lighting, Animation & Dynamics

Learn lighting, rigging, 3D character animation, dynamics and motion graphics.

6 MONTHS
MODULE 04

Advanced Dynamics & Visual Effects

Develop advanced skills in simulations, fire and smoke effects, and digital compositing.

6 MONTHS
MODULE 05

Elective Specialisation

Choose your area of focus: Modelling & Texturing, Lighting & Shading, Character Animation, or Visual Effects.

3 MONTHS

The programme is 27 months long, divided into five structured learning modules.

You will learn drawing, character design, 3D modelling, texturing, rigging, animation, lighting, dynamics and visual effects.

The curriculum includes Autodesk Maya, Adobe Photoshop, Illustrator, Premiere Pro, After Effects, Arnold, Nuke, Substance Painter and other specialised tools.

You can choose one of four specialisations: Modelling & Texturing, Lighting & Shading, Character Animation or Visual Effects.

You can explore career paths such as 3D Modelling Artist, Character Animator, Texture Artist, Lighting Artist, VFX Artist, and Compositing Artist.
